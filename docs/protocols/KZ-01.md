# KZ-01 — Gyroscope / apparent-weight replication protocol

**Status: DRAFT — NOT PREREGISTERED; no experiment has been run.** This is a modern, falsifiable test of a reported mechanical observation, not a test that time itself has been altered.

## Research question
Does a rotating gyroscope produce a reproducible change in a calibrated balance reading compared with matched motor-off and motor-only controls?

## Conditions
1. `motor_off`: rotor stationary, apparatus and mass in place.
2. `rotor_spinning`: rotor at a pre-defined, logged speed.
3. `motor_only_rotor_locked`: motor energized while rotor is mechanically locked, to estimate electrical/thermal/magnetic artifacts.
4. `static_mass`: matched mass without energized apparatus.

Condition order is randomized in balanced blocks. The operator cannot always be blinded to motor state; the analyst must remain blinded to coded condition labels until preprocessing and exclusions are frozen.

## Primary endpoint
Within-block difference in mean balance reading, in milligrams: spinning minus motor-off. Record all raw readings and rotor RPM. Define the minimum effect of interest and equivalence margin from instrument calibration and pilot noise **before** the confirmatory run; do not select them after viewing condition differences.

## Instrumentation and covariates
Calibrated balance with traceable calibration weights; independent vibration sensor; temperature probes at balance, motor and room; magnetometer/EM probe; airflow monitor; inclinometer; acoustic logging; rotor tachometer. Log timestamps, operator, apparatus configuration and calibration checks.

## Run plan
Draft target: 40 randomized blocks total, 10 per condition; 60 repeated readings per block after a fixed stabilization interval. Pilot data are for variance estimation only and must not be pooled into confirmatory analysis. Freeze the final sample size, speed, stabilization time, exclusion rules and statistical model before registration.

## Analysis
Primary analysis: paired/block-aware model comparing spinning with motor-off. Report estimate in mg, 95% confidence interval, exact p-value, equivalence analysis against the pre-specified margin, all exclusions, and raw de-identified data. Secondary comparisons: motor-only and static-mass controls. Correct secondary outcomes for multiplicity. A nominal p<0.05 alone is not evidence of a time-related effect.

## Exclusions and stopping
Exclude only pre-defined sensor failures, failed calibration, safety interruptions, or environmental excursions beyond limits set from pilot characterization. Do not stop early for a favorable result. Record all runs, including null and failed runs.

## Safety
Use a rated rotor enclosure, secure mounting, remote start/stop, overspeed protection and emergency stop. No exposed high-speed rotor, improvised pressure vessel, or human exposure to hazardous fields or mechanical energy.

## Preregistration checklist
- [ ] Apparatus drawings, bill of materials and calibration certificates attached
- [ ] Pilot variance and minimum effect/equivalence margin justified
- [ ] RPM, stabilization interval, block schedule and randomization seed frozen
- [ ] Environmental thresholds and exclusions frozen
- [ ] Analysis code and data dictionary archived with hashes
- [ ] Independent reviewer signs protocol before confirmatory data collection
- [ ] Public timestamped preregistration completed; status changed from `DRAFT_NOT_PREREGISTERED` only after external registration is verified

## Interpretation gate
A positive balance shift first indicates an unexplained mechanical/instrumental effect. A claim about a novel property of time requires independent replication with different apparatus, exclusion of ordinary forces and artifacts, and a separate theoretical account. This protocol does not authorize such a claim by itself.
